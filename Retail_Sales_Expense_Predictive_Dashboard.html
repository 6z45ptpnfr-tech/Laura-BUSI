Automatically generated from current KPIs, diagnostics, model output and your what-if scenario — updates live as you move the sliders.

    Methodology & Assumptions

    • All figures are aggregated network-wide (across all stores and categories) at weekly granularity unless otherwise labelled.
    • Sales and Total Expense are each modelled with an ordinary least squares multiple regression fitted on 80% of weeks (chronological) and validated on the remaining held-out 20%.
    • Sales drivers: total foot traffic, average conversion rate, total marketing spend, average discount rate, average transaction value, consumer confidence index, holiday flag.
    • Expense drivers: total labour hours, average hourly wage, average COGS rate, total marketing spend, modelled sales revenue, total rent expense, total utilities expense.
    • 12-week forward driver values are extrapolated with independent linear trend lines per driver; holiday flags are inferred from historically holiday-flagged week numbers.
    • Prediction intervals use ±1.96 × held-out RMSE (approximate 95% interval under a normal error assumption).
    • What-if scenarios apply slider-adjusted drivers to the same fitted regression coefficients; non-slider drivers are held at their trailing 4-week average.
    • Success probabilities use a normal approximation on the distance between the scenario/forecast value and the target, scaled by held-out RMSE.
